## 🚀 How to Use New Features

### View Statistics
- Automatically displayed on dashboard
- Updates when jobs load

### Use Filters
1. Click filter icon (top right of jobs card)
2. Select locations, job types, or match score
3. Click "Apply Filters"
4. Jobs update automatically

### Save Favorites
1. Click heart icon on any job card
2. View favorites from header button
3. Favorites persist across sessions

### View Job Details
1. Click on any job card
2. Or click "Details" button
3. Full information in modal
4. Click outside or "Close" to dismiss

### Search Jobs
1. Type in search bar
2. Press Enter or click Search
3. Results update in real-time
